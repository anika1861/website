Writing and speaking

Publication

MadPharma

Long-form analyses of drug-safety failures and their regulatory lessons. Each piece reconstructs what went wrong, why the system let it happen, and which rules now stand in the way. The aim is prevention.

Stories so far include the contaminated factor VIII concentrates given to people with haemophilia, and diethylene glycol poisonings from Panama in 2006 to the cough syrup deaths of 2022 and 2023.

Research

Human–AI decision-making

Two preprints on SSRN, July 2026.

  • The Fallible Collaborator: Reconciling Human Tolerance of AI Errors versus Human Errors in Decision-making Systems. Why we forgive a human colleague’s mistakes more readily than an AI system’s, and what that means for decision-making systems.
  • The Inner Game of Hybrid Intelligence: A Mathematical Framework for Overcoming Mirage and Friction in Human-AI Teams. A formal model of what helps and what hinders teams of people and AI systems.
