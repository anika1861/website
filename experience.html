Experience

Fourteen years in clinical research, at sponsors and at CROs. Most recent first.

    Clinical Trial Manager

    Indications: systemic lupus erythematosus, CAR-T (Phase II)

    • Operational and budget lead for complex CAR-T (ATMP) clinical trials. I lead and mentor the cross-functional study team.

  2. Jun 2021 – Jan 2026

    Clinical Trial Lead

    IQVIA, contract research organisation

    Indications: Wilson disease (Phase I), schizophrenia (Phase III), narcolepsy (Phase III), Alzheimer’s disease (Phase I)

    • Identified a caffeine restriction as a critical protocol barrier. Operational and cultural risk data led the medical team to remove it, which secured a top-enrolling site.
    • Traced excess haemolysed central-lab samples to pre-analytical handling (centrifugation and transit temperature), an artefact that distorts safety labs such as potassium and AST. The fix restored data quality and averted a projected 18% budget overrun.
    • Led a team on high-risk requests for information under the EU Clinical Trials Regulation from France and Spain. A cross-country consistency matrix kept every response on time and aligned, and the study was approved.
    • Prepared the team for a sponsor audit that closed in three days with no major or minor findings.
    • When the war in Ukraine broke out, secured sponsor approval within 24 hours and moved all original investigator site files from Kyiv to Poland with zero data loss.

  3. Feb 2020 – May 2021

    CRO Oversight Lead

    Alexion Pharmaceuticals, rare disease

    Indications: Wilson disease, a metabolic liver disease (Phase III); paroxysmal nocturnal haemoglobinuria (Phase III)

    • Introduced a weekly TMF scorecard and accountability calls that raised completeness to 97% ahead of schedule and passed a pre-inspection QA review.
